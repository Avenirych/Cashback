## Partial operations and recovery

`node docker/local-registration/Reconcile.cjs` reports the current missing trusted
composition and stops. For an independently approved existing ledger directory,
the existing read-only tool is `node scripts/registration-infrastructure.cjs
inspect-ledger <protected-directory>`; do not create a ledger to make inspection pass.
Fresh scoped Docker inspection as in step 1 establishes which resources exist;
also inspect the exact attested helper/network IDs once known. Unknown or extra
peers, digest/ports/mount drift, changed daemon, missing anchors or missing fencing
are BLOCKED, never automatically repaired.

Partial create with no durable result: preserve resources and journal, revoke
eligibility and require independent reconciliation. Do not infer success from
names or silently adopt a resource. No automatic create retry. Nonce and receipt
spending remain in the existing durable ledger; an in-memory replay cache cannot
authorize recovery. Writer crash: preserve lock/torn-tail evidence and stop;
never steal an expired lock. mTLS/fencing loss: stop requests, revoke leases and
do not begin work. Unknown COMMIT: retain pending outcome, require read-only
reconciliation, never replay migration. Partial roles/HBA/ACL are outside this
deployment stage and must not be repaired by these scripts.

Never use down -v, prune, bulk deletion, PG recreation or automatic rollback.
Old `cashback-docker-test` and `cashback-finance-security-9f` resources are excluded
from every action; their presence in inventory grants no access.
