## 3. NEW APPROVED RULE — PARTIAL LOT SELECTION

POLICY:
PARTIAL_LOT_SELECTION_FIFO_V1

OWNER DECISION:

Use the earliest confirmed available monetary lots first.

If confirmation timestamps are equal and the allocation remains ambiguous, return pending_review.

IMPLEMENTATION:

Apply this ordering consistently wherever the current approved operation requires choosing a partial amount from several eligible monetary lots, including:

- Customer debt recovery.
- Purchase bonus reservation/application.
- Partial release of reserved bonus.
- Full and partial refund corrections.
- Restoring previously recovered funds where source selection is required.
- Eligible payout holds.

Use the original verified confirmation timestamp associated with the monetary lot.

Internal transfers must not reset the original age.

Exclude funds that are:

- Unconfirmed.
- Restricted.
- Already consumed.
- Reserved for unrelated obligations.
- Owned by another person.
- Otherwise ineligible for the specific operation.

Do not treat FIFO as permission to cross ownership, origin, merchant, debt or reservation restrictions.

When timestamps differ, allocate oldest eligible lots first.

Allow partial consumption of a lot only through an atomic, ledger-backed split or equivalent existing mechanism that preserves provenance, remaining amount and original confirmation timestamp.

When equal timestamps create a materially ambiguous source choice, block that allocation with pending_review.

Do not invent a secondary ordering by row ID, UUID, insertion order or callback arrival time.

Prevent negative balances, duplicate splits, repeated consumption and overcollection.

## 4. NEW APPROVED RULE — MIXED-AGE PAYOUT BELOW £10

POLICY:
SIX_MONTH_MATURE_LOTS_ONLY_V1

OWNER DECISION:

For balances below £10, pay only free eligible monetary lots that have individually reached six calendar months.

Do not include younger lots.

All other payout gates remain mandatory.

IMPLEMENTATION:

For each eligible monetary lot:

- Use its original verified confirmation timestamp.
- Calculate six UTC calendar months.
- Clamp month-end to the last valid day where necessary.
- Preserve the time of day and available timestamp precision.
- Do not substitute 180 days.
- Preserve age across transfers.

Calculate:

- Total free eligible monetary balance.
- Mature free eligible balance.
- Younger free eligible balance.
- Next relevant maturity date.
- Amount eligible for the six-month trigger.

For the six-month trigger, reserve and pay only the mature eligible portion.

Example:

Customer has:
- £4 confirmed seven months ago.
- £3 confirmed one month ago.

The six-month trigger applies only to £4.

The younger £3 remains the customer's liability and must not be consumed by that payout.

IMPORTANT:

The previously approved £10 trigger remains separate.

Do not change the established £10-trigger eligibility rules merely because the six-month trigger is lot-specific.

Both payout triggers retain:

- No disqualifying debt.
- Confirmed ownership and coverage.
- No restrictions.
- Verified recipient/provider.
- Full separately funded platform fee.
- Principal strictly greater than the complete verified fee.
- Atomic principal and fee holds.
- Idempotency.
- Unknown-outcome reconciliation before retry.

A mature £4 amount is not automatically payable if its verified fee or another mandatory gate blocks payment.

Preserve all remaining customer liabilities if payout cannot proceed.

## 5. NEW APPROVED RULE — DOWNWARD RECOVERY CORRECTION

POLICY:
RECOVERY_TARGET_DECREASE_V1

OWNER DECISION:

When the merchant confirms a revised commission that reduces the amount previously recoverable from the buyer:

1. Reduce the still-outstanding debt first.
2. Then restore any funds actually recovered in excess of the revised target.
3. Restore funds to the original owners/lots with provenance preserved.

IMPLEMENTATION:

Reconcile the original purchase, verified commission revisions, refund events, cashback journals, recovery journals, debt obligations and source-lot allocations.

Compute the new cumulative recovery target using the existing approved customer entitlement and rounding rules.

Compare:

- Original recovery target.
- Revised recovery target.
- Amount already collected.
- Amount still outstanding.
- Amount previously restored.

Never treat an outstanding unpaid debt as if it were money already collected.

Cancel or reduce unpaid debt first.

Only restore money actually recovered beyond the revised cumulative target.

Use immutable linked correcting entries.

Preserve:

- Original owner.
- Original source-lot identity or auditable split lineage.
- Original confirmation age.
- Monetary ownership.
- Original restrictions where applicable.

Do not manufacture new confirmed funds or erase prior journal entries.

Do not automatically release funds into an unrestricted available balance if their original restrictions or related obligations remain active.

If exact original source ownership cannot be reconciled, return pending_review.

Handle multiple sequential commission revisions, including:

- Partial refund followed by increased commission.
- Several downward recovery-target changes.
- Later full refund.
- Replayed callbacks.
- Already repaid debt.
- Partially collected debt.
- Previously restored amounts.

Maintain a capped, idempotent cumulative correction model.
