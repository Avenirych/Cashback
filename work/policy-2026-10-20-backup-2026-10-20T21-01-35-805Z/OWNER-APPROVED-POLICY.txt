## 3. PREVIOUSLY APPROVED PURCHASE SELECTION RULES

- Cashback-ineligible products.
- Other excluded line items.

## 4. Q1 — FINAL PURCHASE GROUP SELECTION

Approved:

Select the final winning order only after the seller confirms completion of the entire relevant order group AND applicable finality requirements have been met.

Requirements:

- Trusted seller/group completion evidence.
- All relevant canonical orders considered.
- Verified order eligibility.
- Applicable return/finality conditions satisfied.
- No guessed fixed waiting period.
- No universal arbitrary TTL.
- Missing or contradictory evidence results in pending_review.

Do not finalize from the first callback alone.

## 5. Q2 — LATE HIGHER-VALUE ORDERS

Approved:

After final application, do not silently reassign the bonus.

Use review and linked correcting entries where required.

Requirements:

- Preserve immutable original winner evidence.
- Record later events and their versions.
- Prevent double bonus consumption.

- Never mutate old journal entries.

## 6. Q3 — CANCELLED WINNING ORDER

Approved owner choice B:

Always return the bonus to the buyer when the selected winning order is cancelled.

Do not automatically transfer that bonus to the next order.

Implementation:

Before final bonus consumption:
- Verify cancellation.
- Release the affected reservation back to the buyer's eligible available balance.
- Preserve original lot identity, source conditions and confirmation age.
- Prevent duplicate release.

After bonus consumption:
- Use an immutable correcting transaction.
- Restore only the amount the buyer is entitled to receive.
- Avoid creating money twice.
- If the financial reversal requires recovery of previously spent value, use the existing protected debt/reconciliation machinery.
- Never debit unrelated third-party funds.

If the precise financial state cannot be reconciled, retain pending_review rather than crediting money speculatively.

Do not erase the original purchase or settlement history.
